Time Zone Converter

Popular Time Zones

Offsets are shown against UTC. Many regions change their offset in summer (daylight saving time).

UTC

Coordinated Universal Time (UTC+0)

The global reference used to define all other time zones. It never changes for daylight saving.

GMT

Greenwich Mean Time (UTC+0)

The mean solar time at Greenwich. The UK uses it in winter and switches to BST (UTC+1) in summer.

CET

Central European Time (UTC+1)

Used in much of Europe in winter. In summer, these countries move to CEST (UTC+2).

EST

Eastern Standard Time (UTC−5)

Used in eastern US and Canada in winter. In summer it becomes EDT (UTC−4).

PST

Pacific Standard Time (UTC−8)

Used on the US and Canada west coast in winter. In summer it becomes PDT (UTC−7).

JST

Japan Standard Time (UTC+9)

The time zone of Japan. Japan does not use daylight saving time.

AEST

Australian Eastern Standard Time (UTC+10)

Used on Australia’s east coast. Some states switch to AEDT (UTC+11) in summer; Queensland does not.

About WorldClock

About

WorldClock shows the current time in cities worldwide and converts times between time zones. Everything runs in your browser using the built-in Intl API, so daylight saving time is handled for you.

Privacy Policy
